Hey, I'm Daniel! I poke around inside language models for a living. Well, for a PhD. Two of them, technically, split between [Milano-Bicocca](https://en.unimib.it/) and [Groningen](https://rug.nl/), with [Elisabetta Fersini](https://www.unimib.it/elisabetta-fersini) and [Malvina Nissim](https://www.rug.nl/staff/m.nissim/?lang=en) keeping me on track.

The short version: I open the black box, figure out what's going on in there, and use that to make models behave better. More reliable, easier to steer, a bit smarter. Lately that includes the agents everyone keeps building on top of them.
